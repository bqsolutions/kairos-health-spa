Our Foundation

Where science meets stillness

Founded and operated by Dr. Rebecca Harris, PhD in Physiology from East Carolina University, Kairos Float & Wellness Studio is the first and only sensory deprivation float studio in eastern North Carolina.

We believe that optimal wellness lives at the intersection of cutting-edge research and genuine human care — every service we offer is chosen for its documented benefits, not trends.

Our Services

Five science-backed modalities. One destination. Choose your path to restoration.

01

Float Therapy

Effortlessly suspend in 1,500 lbs of Epsom salt in a private, soundproof suite. 30, 60, 90, and 120-minute sessions. You control the lights and sound — total REST.

Sensory Deprivation
02

Infrared Sauna

Deep tissue warmth using infrared and red light. Detoxify, ease muscle tension, and improve circulation in our full-spectrum infrared sauna suite.

Recovery + Detox
03

Halotherapy

Breathe in micro-particles of pure dry salt in our tranquil salt room. 45-minute sessions. Proven benefits for respiratory health, skin, and deep relaxation.

Salt Therapy
04

Cold Plunge

Immerse in cold water therapy to reduce inflammation, boost mood, and accelerate recovery. A powerful reset for both body and mind.

Cryotherapy
05

Contrast Therapy

Cycle between heat and cold to supercharge recovery. Combine infrared sauna with cold plunge for a powerful circulatory and nervous system reset.
